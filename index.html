How this forecast is built

  • Models: HRRR (3 km, 0–48 h), NBM (2.5 km, ~11 days), ECMWF IFS (9 km, ~15 days), GFS, Canadian GEM, DWD ICON and UK Met Office. Each hour is a weighted average of the models that reach that far out.
  • Calibration: each model's archived forecasts were checked against what the nearby station actually measured over the last three winters. Each model gets a correction for how much it typically under- or over-forecasts here, and a weight based on how well it tracked real storms.
  • Snow ratio: snow water is turned into inches of snow using a temperature-based ratio (Kuchera method), reduced for strong winds.
  • Range: 82 ensemble runs (GFS + ECMWF ensembles) give the 10th–90th percentile spread, scaled to the blend.
  • History: station snow depth from NRCS SNOTEL (US) or Environment Canada (Whistler). New snow is estimated from depth gains, which runs a little low because fresh snow settles.
